# Shop images

`shop-products.png` was created with the built-in image generation tool for this project. It is one 4-column, 2-row product atlas. The shop uses CSS to show each product from its original cell; the image is not cropped or altered. Product indices match `SHOP_ITEMS` in `src/game/shop.ts`.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: a single wide 2:1 product sprite atlas for a dark esports management game shop. EXACTLY 8 equal square cells arranged in a perfectly regular 4-column by 2-row grid, with no gutters, no frames, no text, no labels, no logos, no people, no watermark. Every cell has an identical dark charcoal navy studio background and one fully visible isolated product centered with generous breathing room and subtle red accent lighting. Premium realistic 3D product rendering, clean studio materials, soft reflections, consistent isometric three-quarter camera and scale. Row 1, left to right: 1 black professional gaming headset with a microphone and restrained red details; 2 black ergonomic esports chair with red stitching, entire chair visible; 3 black precision gaming mouse on a small black mousepad, red edge accents; 4 high refresh rate desktop monitor showing an abstract dark tactical map with red shapes, no legible text. Row 2, left to right: 5 black tactical coaching tablet on a subtle stand showing abstract strategy arrows and circles; 6 black scouting laptop showing abstract scouting profiles and analytics, no text; 7 an academy training workstation, compact black desktop computer tower with a small screen and keyboard; 8 a compact black sports recovery kit with a foam roller, massage gun and two exercise bands as a coherent single product kit. Products should fit fully inside their own square cells, with consistent studio backgrounds and lighting. Keep exact grid positions and the whole atlas 2:1.
